You are given a polynomial expression in the variable x. Your task is to fully simplify it by expanding all products and collecting like terms, writing the result as a single polynomial in standard form (terms ordered by descending degree).

Output format rules:
- Use * between every coefficient and variable: write 3*x^2, not 3x^2
- Use ^ for exponents: write x^3, not x³ or x**3
- Omit the coefficient for terms where it equals 1: write x^2, not 1*x^2
- Use + and - between terms with a single space on each side

Example:
Expression: (2*x + 3) * (x + 4) - 5*x

Step 1: 2*x^2 + 8*x + 3*x + 12 - 5*x
Step 2: 2*x^2 + (8 + 3 - 5)*x + 12
Answer: \boxed{{2*x^2 + 6*x + 12}}
